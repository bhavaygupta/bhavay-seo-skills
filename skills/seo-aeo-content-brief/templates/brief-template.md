*Primary keyword is the one query the page must win.*
*Secondary keywords are 3 to 8 close variants and sub-questions that share the same SERP.*
*Market and language, for example US English.*
*Volume and difficulty come from a keyword tool with the tool named. Write "not available" when no tool is connected. Never estimate.*
*Search intent is informational, commercial, transactional or navigational, judged from the SERP, not the keyword alone.*
*Core AI prompt is the question a buyer would type into ChatGPT or Perplexity for this topic.*

## Page type and angle

- **Page type the SERP rewards.**
- **Angle that beats it.**
- **Format check.**

*Page type is guide, listicle, comparison, landing page, tool or glossary, with how many of the top 10 use it.*
*Angle is the one thing this page does that the ranking pages do not, grounded in the optional-angle topics or the brand's real proof.*
*Format check says whether the brand can credibly produce this format. If not, stop and say so.*
